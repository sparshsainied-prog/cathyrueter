Book One, Games We Play Mystery series

Murder May I

Is the killer hiding behind a mask of familiarity?

Dallas clothing designer, Jennifer Clark, wants to get through the next show before her style house's respected reputation is tarnished by further mishaps. But someone is playing dangerous games at the expense of Jen and the Lilac Lane Fashions family. When the scheme turns murderous, beautiful Caribbean Marshall, Jen's best friend and lead model, falls victim. Cari's children, nine-year-old twins, August and January, are caught in the snare. Did they witness their mother's demise … or worse?

To complicate matters, Colin, Cari's brother and Jen's former crush, arrives on the scene. Devastated that her best friend kept secrets, Jen questions her future with the kids and the tenuous control she's gained over her own tumultuous past. The one certain thing in her life is her faith … or is it? When the twisted rules change once again, Jen and Colin must work together to save the twins and win the deadly game of murdered model.

Published by Elk Lake Publishing, Inc., end of May 2025. Print and ebook.

About Cathy

Michigan roots and Texas shoots. She calls herself a Texagander Gal.

Born just outside Chicago and mostly raised in Michigan, Cathy now writes from Texas. She holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts from Grand Valley State University, and she describes her writing as “not-quite-cozy” Christian murder mysteries.

She's a boy mom who had to learn about snakes, reptiles, bugs and airplanes. For years she drove a minivan to haul her boys' gear and a couple of Bearded Dragons, both leash-trained by her sons. She says she can get lost in her own backyard, and that backyard was 22 acres of dense woods.

Her first novel took nearly thirteen years, on and off. In 2023 she signed a three-book contract with Elk Lake Publishing, and she was a finalist in the ACFW Genesis contest.
